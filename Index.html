How each day is checked

Card sales per store and day are taken from the ABO report: Debit column for debit, and all card brands added together for credit. The bank amount for a day is the merchant deposit whose descriptor date is the next day, matched to the store by merchant ID.

When the two differ, the app looks at the time of each sale. The processor closes the day's batch at about 8:30 PM, so sales after that land in the next day's deposit. Differences fully explained that way, or that cancel out over the next few days, are marked as timing and get a written comment. Whatever is left goes to the review list, along with chargebacks, adjustments and held funds from the bank file.
